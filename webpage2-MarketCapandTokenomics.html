The Market Cap and Tokenomic Equation.

The Market Cap of a company is the amound of money they have on record. When you add your money to a company with a low market cap you have the oppurtunity to multiple the amount of money that you contributed.

The Tokenomics of a company is the total supply of tokens available. When the supply of the token circulates, the price for each token equals the total supply divided by the market cap.

Companies can have a market cap from the thousands, to millions, to billions, and forecasted trillions of dollars. All forecasts are using a market cap of 1 tillion dollars.

If a company has a 1 billion token max supply; the forecasted price of one token using a 1 trillion dollar market cap is going to be one thousand dollars. If a company has a 10 billion token max supply; the forecasted price of one token using a 1 trillion dollar market cap is going to be one hundred dollars. If a company has a 100 billion token max supply; the forecasted price of one token using a 1 trillion dollar market cap is going to be ten dollars.
